Foundation emulators

Foundation emulators are models pretrained across many systems that forecast an unseen one. Given a short window of the states of the new system, the model infers which system it is and how it behaves, and predicts the next states; fine-tuning adapts it further.

Φ⋆=argΦmin​ Es∼w​Eu∼μs​​​Φ(ut−k:t​)−ut+1:t+m​​2

Φ is the emulator, s a family drawn with weight w from the pretraining mixture, μs​ its distribution of trajectories, ut−k:t​ the window of k+1 states the model conditions on and ut+1:t+m​ the states to predict. Given are trajectories from many families, with coefficients and forcing varying and never recorded; at query time a window of an unseen system, sometimes with boundary masks. Missing is which system it is, its λ and f, inferred from the window. Sought are the next states.

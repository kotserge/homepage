Super-resolution and downscaling concerns the recovery of the small-scale dynamics from a coarse view of a field. Hence, one cares to learn a map or a conditional distribution from pairs of coarse and fine fields, to add these fine-scales consistent with the coarse view. More formally, let H(u) be the coarse field produced by a degradation H of the fine field u. Hence, we seek to estimate

S⋆=argSmin​ E(H(u),u)​​S(H(u))−u​2,

where S is the map back to the fine field. Since the preimage {u:H(u)} is large, the inverse is one-to-many, and the regressor above returns only the blurred mean E[u∣H(u)]. The more faithful statement is therefore distributional,

q⋆=argqmin​ E[−logq(u∣H(u))],

where q approximates the conditional p(u∣H(u)) of fine fields consistent with one coarse field, and samples from it are sought rather than one field.

Policy learning is the extension of the control & design problem towards the control of a system in a closed loop from sensor readings. This becomes relevant, when a system must be steered in real time, under disturbances and from (partial) measurements, where precomputed inputs would not be sufficient (i.e., such as in control & design settings). We therefore learn a policy that maps readings to actions from many episodes of interaction with an environment, i.e., we seek

ϕ⋆=argϕmax​ Eπϕ​​[t∑​γtrt​],ct​=πϕ​(yt​),ut+1​∼P(⋅∣ut​,ct​),

where πϕ​ is the policy, yt​=H(ut​)+εt​ the noisy sensor readings, ct​ the action, P the transition kernel of the environment (with N, λ and b inside it), rt​ the reward, with ∑t​γtrt​ playing the role of −J, and γ the discount. The environment can be queried, but neither the gradients of N nor the full state are available; the episodes are generated by interaction.

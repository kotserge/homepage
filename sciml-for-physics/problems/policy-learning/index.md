# Policy Learning
---
<div class="card-profile"></div>

Policy learning is the extension of the [control & design](/sciml-for-physics/problems/control-and-design/) problem towards the ==control of a system in a closed loop from sensor readings==. This becomes relevant, when a system must be steered in real time, under disturbances and from (partial) measurements, where precomputed inputs would not be sufficient (i.e., such as in control & design settings). We therefore learn a policy that maps readings to actions from many episodes of interaction with an environment, i.e., we seek
 
$$
\phi^\star=\arg\max_\phi\ \mathbb E_{\pi_\phi}\Big[\sum_{t}\gamma^{t}\,r_t\Big],\qquad c_t=\pi_\phi(y_t),\quad u_{t+1}\sim P(\,\cdot\mid u_t,c_t),
$$

where $\pi_\phi$ is the policy, $y_t=\mathcal H(u_t)+\varepsilon_t$ the noisy sensor readings, $c_t$ the action, $P$ the transition kernel of the environment (with $\mathcal N$, $\lambda$ and $b$ inside it), $r_t$ the reward, with $\sum_t\gamma^t r_t$ playing the role of $-J$, and $\gamma$ the discount. The environment can be queried, but neither the gradients of $\mathcal N$ nor the full state are available; the episodes are generated by interaction.

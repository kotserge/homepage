Classical methods, e.g., Finite Difference, Finite Element, Finite Volume or Spectral Methods, discretize the continuous problem into a finite-dimensional system

Nh​[uh​;λ]=fh​

where Nh​ and fh​ represent the operator and forcing function after discretization and uh​ is the corresponding discrete solution (grid values, cell averages or basis coefficients) for the given system under its coefficients λ.

Physics-Informed Neural Networks. Similar to the classical approach, the physics-informed method requires full knowledge about the physics. However, in contrast to the classical approach, where the equations are the model, here, they serve to inform the trained model of its accuracy.

Physics-informed operator learning

PI-DeepONet and PINO train an operator network on the residual over a family of inputs rather than on one instance. The equations hold for every input drawn from the family, so sampling inputs and enforcing the residual on each trains the solution map without any solved data; in PINO a few solutions can be mixed in.

θmin​ Ea∼μ​[​N[Gθ​(a);a]−fa​​2+β​B[Gθ​(a)]−ba​​2]

Gθ​ is the operator network, a∼μ an input of the family (the varying coefficients, forcing or boundary data), and fa​, ba​ the forcing and boundary data of that input. The method requires the operator explicitly and the input distribution; derivatives of Gθ​(a) come from automatic differentiation or, in PINO, from Fourier differentiation on the grid.

Data
Sampled inputs ai​∼μ; no solutions, or a few.
Model
DeepONet or FNO; the residual is evaluated on the output.
Optimization
Adam on the expected residual; instance-wise fine-tuning at test time in PINO.

Where the physics enters The loss, as the residual over the family.

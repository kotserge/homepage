Physics-Informed'ness in training models acts as a supervisory signal for these models, based on the physics residuals of the predicted solution with respect to the underlying equation, boundary data, and initial state:

θmin​ ​N[uθ​;λ]−f​Ω×(0,T]2​+βb​​B[uθ​]−b​∂Ω2​+β0​​uθ​(⋅,0)−u0​​2,

on the domain Ω over (0,T], N the operator with coefficients λ, f the forcing, B the boundary operator with data b, and u0​ the initial state. While uθ​ is the network learning the solution, the key point is that the definition of physics-informed'ness is independent of the choice of u, but it enforces the governing dynamics on the model choice through the loss definition.

01

Nearby methods

Classical Discretization Methods. In Physics-Informed Neural Networks, the learned solver’s correctness is enforced via the governing equations, whereas in a classical view, the solver enforces the physical constraints.

Physics-Informed Operator Learning. Because physics-informedness is a supervisory signal, it can be extended to enforce correctness over a family of coefficients and forcings rather than a single fixed instance, allowing the training of so-called Neural Operators.

02

Problems addressed

Forward Problem. The Physics-Informed signal in combination with a neural network for point prediction is historically the classical approach on solving the forward problem by neural networds. It gives everything the method needs.

Inverse Problem Next to the network, one can learn the coefficient of the system while learning the solution u. However, this requires data of the instance in question, else the probelm becomes ill-defined.
